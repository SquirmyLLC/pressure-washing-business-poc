FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will pressure washing damage my siding or roof?

No. Siding and roofs get a low-pressure soft wash with cleaners that do the work. High pressure is only used on concrete and stone.

Do I need to be home?

Not for outdoor jobs. We just need access to an outdoor water spigot, and we text before and after photos.

Are your cleaners safe for plants and pets?

Yes. We use biodegradable cleaners and pre-wet and rinse your plants. Pets should stay inside until surfaces dry.

How long does it take?

Most driveways take 1 to 2 hours and a full house wash takes half a day.

How often should I get it done?

Once a year keeps most homes looking sharp. Shady, damp sides may need it every 6 months.

Are you insured?

Fully licensed and insured, with proof sent on request before any job.
